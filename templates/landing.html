Estonia Company Finder

A small internal web app for company research against Estonia's official e-Business Register (ariregister.rik.ee) and its public open-data.

It lets an authorised team member:

  • search the e-Business Register for amending entries over a chosen period and legal form (OÜ, UÜ, TÜ, AS);
  • filter those companies by annual-report revenue;
  • look up a company's revenue by country (domestic vs. export).

Access is limited to team members with a shared login. There is no sign-up, and no data is shared with third parties.
